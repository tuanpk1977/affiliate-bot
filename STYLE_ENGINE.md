# Style Engine

Use this as the writing style contract.

For complete voice rules, also load `WRITING_DNA.md` and `VOICE_DNA.md`.

## Voice

Practical, calm, evidence-led, and buyer-focused. Write like an experienced operator explaining how to avoid a bad software decision.

## Reading Level

Aim for Grade 8-10. Use short paragraphs. Mix sentence length. Avoid inflated phrasing.

## Forbidden Patterns

Avoid:

- In conclusion
- It is important to note
- Nowadays
- In today's digital world
- game changer
- unlock your potential
- seamless experience
- cutting-edge solution
- leverage robust capabilities
- generic claims such as "boost productivity" without context

## Paragraph Rules

Each paragraph must add one new point. If two paragraphs make the same point, merge or delete one.

## Claims

Use cautious language when a source does not prove a claim. Say "appears", "claims", "according to the official page", or "could fit" when certainty is limited.

## Affiliate Tone

Disclose affiliate links plainly. Do not pressure the reader to buy. The CTA should invite deeper review, pricing verification, or comparison.
