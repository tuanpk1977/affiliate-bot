# Structure DNA

This document defines the exact article architecture used by the editorial engine.

## Ordering Rules

1. Title and metadata.
2. H1.
3. Introduction.
4. Affiliate disclosure.
5. Author/editorial signal.
6. Quick answer or quick verdict.
7. Table of contents when the article is long.
8. Methodology or evaluation criteria.
9. Main body sections.
10. Comparison table when multiple options are discussed.
11. Pricing considerations.
12. Features or workflow evaluation.
13. Pros.
14. Cons.
15. Alternatives.
16. Common mistakes.
17. FAQ.
18. Final recommendation.
19. Disclosure/footer.
20. JSON-LD.

## Metadata

Title should normally be under 60 characters when possible. Meta description should be 140-160 characters for SEO companion outputs and present for public pages.

## Introduction

The introduction must establish audience, problem, article scope, and verification limits.

## Quick Answer

Use when the article targets a buyer query. It should answer who the tool/category fits, who should avoid it, and what must be verified.

## Tables

Comparison tables should have concise columns and mobile-safe cells. Use them for alternatives, top lists, and direct comparisons.

## Pricing

Pricing sections must explain what to verify. Do not invent prices.

## FAQ

FAQ should contain at least three buyer questions when schema is expected.

## Schema

Public article output should support Article, BreadcrumbList, Person/Author, Organization, WebSite, and FAQPage when relevant.
