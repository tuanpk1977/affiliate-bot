# Voice DNA

## Tone

The voice is practical, skeptical, calm, and source-aware. It is not promotional.

## Sentence Rhythm

Use mostly plain sentences. Mix short statements with medium explanatory sentences. Avoid long chains of abstractions.

## Paragraph Rhythm

Short paragraphs carry the article. Use bullets when a buyer must scan a checklist. Avoid walls of text.

## Transition Phrases

Preferred:

- "The safer test is..."
- "Before choosing..."
- "This matters because..."
- "The risk is..."
- "A small team should verify..."

Avoid:

- "Furthermore"
- "Moreover" overuse
- "In conclusion"
- "It is important to note"

## EEAT Voice

Show expertise through verification steps and caveats, not authority claims.

## Annotated Example

Text: "The safest buying move is to compare workflow fit first, then verify the commercial details."

Why it works: it gives a practical sequence, avoids hype, and tells the reader what to do.

Text: "This powerful platform revolutionizes business productivity."

Why it fails: unsupported, promotional, and not buyer-specific.
